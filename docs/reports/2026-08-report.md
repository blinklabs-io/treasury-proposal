# Progress Report - August 2026

**Period:** August 1, 2026 - August 31, 2026
**Report Type:** Monthly

## Summary

August continued the Q3 hardening period. Dingo expanded Leios safety and
recovery behavior, Dijkstra ledger and governance support, real-backend
conformance coverage, database lifecycle handling, API compatibility, Mithril
bootstrapping, and block-production safeguards. gOuroboros delivered a large
set of ledger, consensus, protocol, and CBOR validation improvements, while
Plutigo and ouroboros-mock shipped compatibility and test-harness updates.

The local August main-branch history contains 354 Dingo commits (306 excluding
dependency updates), 97 gOuroboros commits (95 excluding dependency updates),
and releases across all four project repositories. Dingo released `v0.69.0`,
`v0.70.0`, `v0.70.1`, and `v0.70.2`; gOuroboros released `v0.191.0` through
`v0.202.3`; Plutigo released `v0.3.0`, `v0.4.0`, and `v0.5.0`; and
ouroboros-mock released `v0.15.1`, `v0.16.0`, and `v0.16.1`.

No August treasury transaction is recorded in this repository's journal. M-4
Infra July and M-5 Infra August reached their scheduled maturity dates, but no
claim is recorded here during the reporting period.

  binding, bounded Endorser Block caches, connection-scoped CertRB waits,
  abandoned transaction-fetch recovery, unavailable-data handling, and
  cross-EB double-consume recovery.

  Plutigo v0.5.0 cost models, phase-two validation during historical replay,
  reward-withdrawal and reward-parameter fixes, monetary-expansion handling,
  governance arithmetic checks, committee term limits, SPO non-voter rules,

  PostgreSQL, and MySQL conformance runs, one corpus replay per backend,
  bounded Badger snapshots and garbage-collection waits, database lifecycle
  lock isolation, immutable-index checks, and safer shutdown and recovery.
- Dingo improved block production and chain recovery by enforcing KES lifetime,
  publishing only accepted blocks, recovering producer-loop panics, pinning
  transaction selection to a validation snapshot, validating Genesis headers,
  bounding Genesis recovery scans, and preserving rollback and Mithril state.
- Dingo improved operator and API coverage with transaction evaluation,
  bounded address pagination and UTxO loading, applied-tip backfill limits,
  optimized preview API bootstrap timing, and bundled-devnet and concurrent
  DevNet reliability fixes.
- gOuroboros strengthened Dijkstra, Conway, Byron, and Shelley-family
  validation, including governance guardrails, certificate deposits and
  refunds, reward-account and withdrawal validation, block-witness counts,
  pool-relay CDDL bounds, validity intervals, wire-CBOR preservation, and

  safer LeiosFetch timeouts, and protocol responder lifecycle fixes.
- Plutigo removed the non-reference final slippage skip, made constructor tags
  variant-aware, and added Plutus program validation. ouroboros-mock exposed
  the server-under-test connection and preserved reward-account state in
  conformance fixtures.

- Leios remains coupled to an evolving prototype and continues to require
  compatibility and safety updates. August's bounded caches, voter-key checks,
  fetch recovery, and certificate-serving changes reduce but do not remove this
  integration risk.
- Storage scalability and long-running correctness remain open risks. Backend
  conformance coverage has expanded, but it does not yet establish production
  performance at mainnet data volumes.
- The security audit has not started in the available records. Auditor
  engagement and scheduling remain a Q3 priority.
- Dijkstra and Plutus V4 behavior is still being integrated across Dingo,
  gOuroboros, and Plutigo, so final hard-fork readiness remains in progress.
