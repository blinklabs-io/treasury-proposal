# Progress Report - July 2026

**Period:** July 1, 2026 - July 31, 2026
**Report Type:** Monthly

## Summary

July was the first month of the Q3 delivery period. Engineering moved from
building the Leios prototype to making it track successive Musashi prototype
revisions while increasing ledger, database, consensus, mempool, and API
coverage at mainnet scale. Dingo also advanced Dijkstra and Plutus V4 support,
including epoch reward processing, CIP-163 delegator inactivity, and a new DAG
mempool implementation.

The local July main-branch record contains 254 Dingo commits (206 excluding
dependency updates), 54 gOuroboros commits, 13 Plutigo commits, and the
`ouroboros-mock` v0.15.0 release. Dingo released `v0.61.1` through `v0.68.0`,
gOuroboros released `v0.186.2` through `v0.190.0`, and Plutigo released
`v0.1.17` and `v0.2.0`.

Treasury operations also began the month with the claim of the three matured
M-1, M-2, and M-3 milestones. No additional treasury transactions were
recorded during July.

## Deliverables This Period

Source basis: July 2026 main-branch history in the local Dingo, gOuroboros,
Plutigo, and ouroboros-mock repositories, plus the transaction journal and
on-chain metadata in this repository.

- Dingo continued the Leios implementation against Musashi prototype revisions
  2026w27 through 2026w30. Endorser Blocks gained separate tracking,
  historical and asynchronous blob-store persistence, pool and governance
  effects, committee parameter updates, voting changes, fetch-stall failover,
  and better CertRB serving. gOuroboros added the matching Leios wire-format
  and prototype compatibility changes.
- Advanced Dijkstra and consensus correctness: Dijkstra forging and inbound
  block-envelope validation, operational-certificate validation, Praos epoch
  nonce handling across Mithril boundaries, VRF eligibility checks, safer
  chain selection and rollback recovery, Genesis fast-source corroboration,
  header forecast-horizon enforcement, and Byron EBB handling.
- Expanded ledger and governance behavior with Shelley reward formulas,
  epoch reward calculation and application, full pot distribution, reward
  stake snapshots, CIP-163 delegator inactivity, CIP-50 pledge leverage,
  CIP-23 minimum pool margin, collateral-fee accounting, and safer governance
  replay and MIR arithmetic.
- Improved mainnet-scale infrastructure: lock-free copy-on-write ledger
  snapshots, configurable FIFO and DAG-based mempools, database connection
  pool limits, SQL dialect sharing, live-stake and reward-state indexes,
  configurable plugin loading, Mithril catch-up and snapshot fixes, and
  PostgreSQL/MySQL conformance runs through the ouroboros-mock harness.
- Expanded the Blockfrost-compatible API with address, DRep, pool, reward
  history, stake-account UTxO, withdrawal, transaction, pool metadata, and
  retiring-pool endpoints, while aligning pool responses with OpenAPI 0.1.90.
  Added startup configuration validation and enforced TLS 1.2 minimums for
  UTxORPC and Bark servers.
- gOuroboros shipped Dijkstra reference-script size limits, stronger handling
  for duplicate and unknown CBOR/ledger encodings, Leios vote and fetch
  protocol updates, stake-address and chain-dependent state queries, and
  stricter Byron and address validation.
- Plutigo synchronized V4 builtins, added a mainnet script replay runner and
  replay corpus, improved CEK environment lookup performance, and implemented
  Keccak-256 without the go-ethereum dependency. ouroboros-mock added DRep
  delegation and an observable ChainSync server-callback test harness.

## Risks and Issues

- Leios remains coupled to an evolving Musashi prototype; July required four
  compatibility updates. This remains the primary specification and integration
  risk and is mitigated by tracking the prototype directly and testing against
  live devnet behavior.
- Storage scalability and long-running correctness remain open risks. The new
  database, mempool, replay, and PostgreSQL/MySQL tests improve evidence but do
  not yet establish mainnet-scale performance.
- The M-10 security audit has not started because an auditor has not yet been
  engaged. The milestone remains funded but Paused; audit scheduling is a Q3
  priority.
- Dijkstra and Plutus V4 behavior is still being integrated across Dingo,
  gOuroboros, and Plutigo, so hard-fork readiness remains in progress.
