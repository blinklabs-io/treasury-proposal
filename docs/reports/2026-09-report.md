# Progress Report - September 2026

**Period:** September 1, 2026 - September 30, 2026  
**Report Type:** Monthly

## Summary

September closed the scheduled Q3 period with continued Dijkstra and Plutus V4
integration, Leios validation, storage and ledger performance work, and expanded
cross-node and conformance tooling. Dingo added Dijkstra committee and block
validation, a Koios-backed from-genesis parity run, database and pipeline
metrics, and a KES agent client. gOuroboros and Plutigo supplied protocol,
ledger, evaluator, and V4 support; ouroboros-mock expanded its all-era fixtures
and Blueprint conformance corpus.

The local `origin/main` histories contain 610 Dingo commits, 240 gOuroboros
commits, 37 Plutigo commits, and 50 ouroboros-mock commits during September.
These totals include dependency updates and related compatibility work.
September release tags were Dingo `v0.70.3` through `v0.75.0`, gOuroboros
`v0.202.4` through `v0.208.5`, Plutigo `v0.5.1` through `v0.8.0`, and
ouroboros-mock `v0.19.0` through `v0.20.4`.

No September treasury transaction appears in this repository's journal. The
scheduled dates for M-6 Q3 Storage and M-7 Infra September were September 30;
the journal does not record a claim for either. M-10 Audit remains funded but
paused, and auditor engagement is not recorded.
