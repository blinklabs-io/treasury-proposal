# Progress Report - May 2026

Period: May 1, 2026 - May 31, 2026
Report type: Monthly

## Summary

May was the month the treasury funding went live. The governance action was
enacted, the 6,900,000 ADA withdrawal landed in the treasury contract, the
working budget was converted to USDCx, vendor milestones were configured and
funded, and the first milestone (M-0 Bootstrap) was claimed. Every step is
recorded in the transparency journal with on-chain metadata.

Engineering kept moving against the Q2 milestone: testnet block production
and the Leios prototype. Dingo spent the month hardening the paths a block
producer actually exercises: forging, era transitions, rollback and fork
recovery, Mithril bootstrap, and peer behavior, while continuing to build out
storage performance and the Blockfrost-compatible API. gOuroboros picked up
initial Dijkstra era support and the Leios voting protocol. Plutigo kept
pushing evaluator performance.

## Treasury Operations

The enacted withdrawal moved on-chain this month. On May 12 the 6,900,000 ADA
was withdrawn from the treasury contract reward account into the treasury
script UTxO. On May 13, 5,630,000 ADA was disbursed to Kraken for an OTC
trade, co-signed by oversight board member Pi Lanningham with a written
attestation of what he verified before signing. The trade executed at 0.2720
USDC per ADA for 1,531,360 USDC. The remaining 900,000 ADA contingency and
370,000 ADA earmarked for vendor milestones never left Cardano.

On May 14 the USDC came back: a 100 USDC test transfer first to verify the
bridge address, then the full 1,531,308 USDC bridged to USDCx on Cardano. On
May 18 the oversight board ran the fund action, configuring Blink Labs as
vendor with 370,000 ADA and 1,528,333 USDCx against the milestone schedule.
Roughly 900,001 ADA and 2,975 USDCx remain in the treasury contract. The M-10
Audit milestone was funded as matured but paused; the board will resume it
once an auditor is engaged.

On May 19 Blink Labs claimed the matured M-0 Bootstrap milestone, 225,000
USDCx, to the operating wallet. Individual journal entries in `journal/`
carry the transaction hashes, signers, and metadata hashes for each of these.

## Treasury Milestone

The relevant treasury milestone is Q2: testnet block production and the Leios
prototype. Block production work in May centered on the things that go wrong
in practice: validating producer credentials at startup, tracking opcert
issue numbers per pool, matching cardano-node's Praos chain selection, and
fixing VRF nonce calculations. Era-transition correctness was proven
end-to-end from Shelley through Conway, with tests crossing vanRossem/PV11.
On the Leios side, Dingo now serves merged Ranking Block and Endorser Block
data over N2C, and gOuroboros gained the LeiosVotes mini-protocol with vote
and certificate handling for the stake-based committee. gOuroboros also
landed initial Dijkstra era support, an early start on the Dijkstra readiness
milestone.

## Dingo

- Shipped 19 May releases from `v0.39.1` through `v0.49.1`.
- Hardened block production: producer credential validation at startup,
  per-pool opcert issue-number tracking for stale-counter checks,
  schedule-aware slot-to-epoch lookups, chain selection matched to
  cardano-node Praos behavior, VRF nonce fixes, and CBOR diagnostics when a
  forged block fails to re-decode.
- Proved era-transition correctness end-to-end from Shelley through Conway,
  including protocol version bumps at transitions, header version checks,
  and new era-transition test plumbing verified across vanRossem/PV11.
- Started serving merged Ranking Block + Endorser Block data over N2C and
  added Leios-mode mempool defaults.
- Improved rollback and fork recovery: ledger state rewind on rollback,
  skipping already-applied headers during local recovery, live rollback
  reconciliation in the plateau watchdog, and better intersect handling
  across sparse history and snapshot gaps.
- Hardened Mithril bootstrap with idle-stall detection and retry/resume,
  safer restore boundaries, sync metrics, and governance roots seeded from
  the snapshot.
- Improved peer behavior: divergent peer handling, post-plateau realignment,
  keeping the only peer through local tip plateaus, automatic peer-sharing
  management, and new peer temperature and protocol-level metrics.
- Continued storage and backfill work: a batch accumulator in the backfill
  pipeline with configurable batch size, reduced backfill memory, genesis
  bootstrap for MySQL and Postgres, and SQLite planner and index tuning.
- Filled out Conway governance handling with CIP-1694 SPO reward-account
  delegation semantics, reward state persistence, and delegations and
  initial DReps from Conway genesis.
- Expanded the Blockfrost-compatible API with account, transaction, and
  governance endpoints, plus CORS support.

## gOuroboros

- Shipped May releases `v0.166.1`, `v0.167.0`, `v0.168.0`, `v0.169.0`,
  `v0.170.0`, `v0.170.1`, `v0.171.0`, `v0.178.0`, and `v0.179.0`.
- Landed initial Dijkstra era support, along with min/max protocol versions
  in era packages, Conway PV9 gating, and the pv10-to-Plomin rename raising
  the maximum protocol version to 11.
- Added the LeiosVotes mini-protocol and vote/Endorser Block certificate
  handling for the stake-based committee.
- Advanced DMQ (CIP-0137) support: N2C connections, alignment with the
  current CIP spec, TTL validation, and expired-message filtering.
- Added the peer-sharing mini-protocol and LocalStateQuery
  `GetLedgerPeerSnapshot`.
- Built a CBOR diagnostics suite with offset tracking and navigation, hex
  dumps, Cardano-aware formatters, and enhanced decode errors.
- Hardened ledger validation: fail-closed phase-2 errors, rejection of
  oversized collateral returns, witness checks for key credential
  withdrawals, and fast-fail on unknown Conway redeemer tags.
- Fixed consensus and connection robustness issues, including canonical
  blake2b VRF key-hash validation and a blockfetch shutdown race, and added
  metadata and message fuzzers.

## Plutigo

- Shipped May releases `v0.1.10` through `v0.1.13`.
- Continued the evaluator performance push with a reverse stack machine
  rewrite, DeBruijn-specialized optimizations, unsafe-tab dispatch with a
  lambda fast path, builtin force/arity caching, and arena allocator tuning.
- Hardened decode and output safety: Plutus data decode limits, an expmod
  budget overflow fix, canonical encoding of empty CBOR bytestrings, and
  control-character escaping in pretty output.
