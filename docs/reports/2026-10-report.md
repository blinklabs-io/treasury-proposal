# Progress Report - October 2026

**Period:** October 1, 2026 - October 31, 2026  
**Report Type:** Monthly

## Summary

October opened the scheduled Q4 period with Dijkstra and Plutus V4 evaluation
in Dingo, continued Leios integration and observability, CIP-0137 DMQ
networking, and a large round of storage, Mithril, and ledger-replay
performance work. Dingo extended its Koios-backed and from-genesis parity
tooling to mainnet, added in-node Mithril sync and snapshot serving, expanded
LocalStateQuery coverage, and added Blockfrost- and Kupo-compatible API
surfaces. gOuroboros aligned Dijkstra with the Leios release candidate and
registered DijkstraNet; Plutigo focused on evaluator performance and a
validated evaluation API; ouroboros-mock added Dijkstra and all-era builders
and reactive node-to-node peers.

The local `origin/main` histories contain 599 Dingo commits, 171 gOuroboros
commits, 45 Plutigo commits, and 29 ouroboros-mock commits during October.
These totals include dependency updates and related compatibility work.
October release tags were Dingo `v0.75.1` through `v0.90.2`, gOuroboros
`v0.209.0` through `v0.216.3`, Plutigo `v0.8.1` through `v0.10.1`, and
ouroboros-mock `v0.20.5` through `v0.21.1`.

On October 16, the Dingo GitHub milestone "Operational Hardening and Storage
Scalability" closed with 119 of 119 issues complete, and Blink Labs claimed
M-4 Infra July, M-5 Infra August, M-6 Q3 Storage, and M-7 Infra September in a
single vendor withdrawal. M-8 Infra October reached its scheduled date on
October 31 and has no recorded claim. M-10 Audit remains paused on-chain.

## Deliverables This Period

Source basis: October 2026 `origin/main` history and release tags in the local
Dingo, gOuroboros, Plutigo, and ouroboros-mock repositories, plus this
repository's transaction journal and milestone metadata.

- Dingo evaluates Dijkstra Plutus V4 scripts and serves their protocol
  parameters, supports DijkstraNet, and synced the Musashi `rc-2026w42`
  configurations and cost models. gOuroboros aligned its Dijkstra ledger with
  the Leios release candidate, added current Dijkstra protocol parameters, and
  exported Dijkstra Plutus script evaluation.
- Dingo advanced Leios with block-transaction endorser-block adoption,
  certification timing and epoch-boundary enforcement, configurable
  persistence retention, a LeiosDB benchmark, and Leios metrics and dashboards.
  gOuroboros prioritized Praos over Leios outbound traffic and narrowed
  `MsgBlockTxs` to the requested transactions.
- Dingo added CIP-0137 DMQ node-to-node message submission, local submission
  and notification, a peer listener and peer manager, ledger peer discovery,
  and batched reply authentication.
- Dingo added consensus provider selection (Praos and Tachys), the Genesis
  Density Disconnector, the Limit on Eagerness for chain selection, trust of
  corroborated Genesis frontiers with equivocation counting, and Peras
  scaffolding with a vector loader.
- Storage and replay work in Dingo included a UTxO overlay for trusted batch
  application, bulk transaction and live-stake writes, block-UTxO prefetch,
  cached and prepared block-apply SQL, Badger memtable and value-log tuning,
  and numerous SQLite query optimizations. Mithril sync now runs inside the
  node, and Dingo can produce, serve, and certify Mithril snapshot artifacts.
- Dingo extended its Koios-backed parity observer and from-genesis node-parity
  run to mainnet, and began publishing release conformance and CBOR reports.
- Ecosystem integration advanced with a Kupo-compatible API, Blockfrost wallet,
  dApp, historical, and governance query operations, additional
  LocalStateQuery answers served from acquired snapshots, an MCP server, a
  one-command local devnet, OpenTelemetry tracing, and file-backed secrets.

## Per-Project Detail

### Dingo

- Shipped `v0.75.1` through `v0.90.2` across 599 October commits.
- Added Dijkstra Plutus V4 evaluation, DijkstraNet support, and Musashi
  `rc-2026w42` configurations and cost models.
- Advanced Leios endorser-block adoption, certification timing, persistence
  retention, metrics, and dashboards; added CIP-0137 DMQ node-to-node
  submission, peer management, and ledger peer discovery.
- Added Praos and Tachys consensus provider selection, Genesis Density
  Disconnector, Limit on Eagerness, and Peras scaffolding.
- Moved Mithril sync into the node, added Mithril snapshot production and
  serving, and substantially optimized replay, Mithril import, SQLite, and
  Badger paths.
- Extended Koios and from-genesis parity validation to mainnet; added Kupo and
  expanded Blockfrost compatibility, more LocalStateQuery answers, an MCP
  server, a one-command devnet, and OpenTelemetry tracing.

### gOuroboros

- Shipped `v0.209.0` through `v0.216.3` across 171 October commits.
- Aligned Dijkstra with the Leios release candidate, registered DijkstraNet,
  added current Dijkstra protocol parameters, and added Tachys leader
  selection.
- Added node-to-client versions 22 and 23, cancelable Plutus evaluation,
  preverified witness signatures, muxer ingress bounds and Praos-over-Leios
  prioritization, and complete UTxO-RPC transaction projections.
- Updated to Plutigo `v0.10.0` and reduced CBOR, header, and script-context
  overhead.

### Plutigo

- Shipped `v0.8.1` through `v0.10.1` across 45 October commits.
- Added a validated evaluation API with cost model checks and metrics.
- Improved FLAT decoding and CEK evaluation performance, including shared
  per-cost-model setup, smaller environment nodes, and fewer builtin
  allocations.

### ouroboros-mock

- Shipped `v0.20.5` through `v0.21.1` across 29 October commits.
- Added Dijkstra transaction and block builders, all-era block and certificate
  builders, complete protocol flow builders, and configurable handshake
  fixtures.
- Added reactive node-to-node peers, scripted YAML listeners, stake-weighted
  Conway ratification and CIP-159 builders, and validated curated SSC and
  script goldens.
